# bothertoken
A simple little PCB toy designed for Hack Club's Solder YSWS. 
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fe6443eb-932e-46e4-a45e-3652ba8df1ae)


This little design uses an NPN transistor, a photoresistor, and a SPST switch to bother all your friends endlessly. When switched on and placed in an environment with low light (like your friend's bag) it'll buzz utilizing the vibrating motor until turned off or brought into light. The light sensitivity can be adjusted using the potentiometer.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2ecbb580-2386-455b-8ed9-6438eb80cce5)


## Bill of Materials:
- Cell Battery
- Photoresistor
- Resistor
- Potentiometer
- NPN Transistor
- Vibrating DC Motor
